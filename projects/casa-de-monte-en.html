Project overview

What this project is — and is not.

Casa de Monte is a private community of 36 exclusive pool villas in Koh Kaew, a quiet inland section of Phuket, deliberately set away from active tourist zones. The territory is delivered with consistent Italian-style architecture and a unified landscape concept by developer The Title.

Layouts are 3- and 4-bedroom pool villas, ranging from 297 to 450 m² built area, in single- and two-storey configurations. The project is positioned for personal residence as the primary use case, with rental yield as a secondary path supported by the management company.

On-site infrastructure includes a swimming pool and children's pool, fitness area, sauna, lobby with public spaces, dedicated coworking, kids' room and playroom, plus parking. Management is performed under a single operating standard for the entire community to preserve common appearance and asset quality.

Tenure: registered leasehold for foreign buyers in standard structures, with freehold paths available subject to project documentation and counsel review. Completion July 2029.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Can a foreigner own a villa at Casa de Monte?
Yes. In Thailand, the building (the house itself) can be owned by a foreign national in their name. The land underneath is typically held through a registered leasehold or a Thai company structure; freehold paths may be available depending on the project documentation. We structure each purchase with independent legal review so ownership is clear and documented.
How does leasehold work — and is it secure?
A registered lease is protected under Thai law for its term. Developers commonly offer a primary term with contractual renewal options. Before signing we review who the lessor is, your rights under the lease, and inheritance/succession provisions, so you understand exactly what you are securing.
What is the price range and what are the villa types?
Five pool-villa types, 297–450 m², indicatively from 28.2M THB (3-bedroom) up to 47.2M THB (4-bedroom, top configuration). Exact pricing per unit and what is included are confirmed in the developer quote. See the pricing & floor plans section above.
How does the payment schedule work?
A reservation deposit (300,000–500,000 THB) secures the unit, followed by 25% on contract signing and staged instalments tied to construction milestones, with the balance at handover. Completion is scheduled for July 2029. Full breakdown in the payment plan section.
What are the ongoing costs of ownership?
Owners pay a monthly common-area maintenance (CAM) fee based on the indoor area, plus utilities at government rates. For new-build projects it is common to prepay 1–3 years of CAM at the transfer of ownership. Exact figures are provided in the project dossier.
Can I pay in my own country and currency?
In many cases, yes. As an independent agent on a direct contract with the developer within a Singapore corporate group with international banking infrastructure, we can arrange settlement to the developer in your own jurisdiction and currency where available — subject to onboarding, KYC/AML, source-of-funds verification and applicable law. WEGC is not a bank or licensed payment institution.
Is the villa suitable for rental?
Casa de Monte is positioned residence-first, with rental as a secondary path supported by a management company. Indicative yields in this inland micro-market are around 5–7% per annum — general guidance only, not a forecast or guarantee.
What is the buying process with WEGC?
Enquiry → onboarding and KYC/AML → unit selection and reservation → sale & purchase agreement / lease → staged payments → snagging → transfer of ownership. Every step is documented, and we act on a direct contract with the developer.
