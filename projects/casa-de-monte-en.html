Project overview

What this project is — and is not.

Casa de Monte is a private community of 36 exclusive pool villas in Koh Kaew, a quiet inland section of Phuket, deliberately set away from active tourist zones. The territory is delivered with consistent Italian-style architecture and a unified landscape concept by developer The Title.

Layouts are 3- and 4-bedroom pool villas, ranging from 297 to 450 m² built area, in single- and two-storey configurations. The project is positioned for personal residence as the primary use case, with rental yield as a secondary path supported by the management company.

On-site infrastructure includes a swimming pool and children's pool, fitness area, sauna, lobby with public spaces, dedicated coworking, kids' room and playroom, plus parking. Management is performed under a single operating standard for the entire community to preserve common appearance and asset quality.

Tenure: registered leasehold for foreign buyers in standard structures, with freehold paths available subject to project documentation and counsel review. Completion July 2029.
