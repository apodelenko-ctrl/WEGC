Project overview

What this project is — and is not.

The Title Biancana is the headline 2026 pre-sale of Rhom Bho Property in the Surin corridor — historically Phuket's most established luxury beach zone, with sunset orientation and panoramic Andaman views.

The scheme is structured as four low-rise buildings sharing a common landscaped territory, with approximately 343 apartments. Layouts range from compact studios to 3-bed residences of 130 m². The project is delivered furnished and finished to a documented business-class standard.

The on-site infrastructure follows a 5★ resort logic: two pools (including a rooftop pool with sea and mountain panoramas), restaurant, large landscaped park, fitness, spa, dedicated coworking, concierge, pet-friendly zoning, underground parking and a partner management company for owners.

Sale is structured as registered long-term leasehold. Indicative entry from USD 155,000; specific layouts, prices and allocation are confirmed under a private offer. Completion December 2028.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Can a foreigner buy here?
Foreign buyers can typically own condominium units under Thailand's freehold foreign-ownership quota, or hold a villa via registered leasehold or a Thai company structure. We confirm the exact path for this project and structure each purchase with documentation and legal review.
What is the starting price and what is included?
We quote the developer's current list price and the furniture/finish package on request — there is no separate buyer-side agency markup. Exact pricing depends on unit, floor and availability.
How do payment and completion work?
A reservation deposit secures the unit, followed by a contract payment and milestone-linked instalments per the developer schedule, with the balance at handover. We share the full schedule and completion date in the dossier.
Can I pay in my own country and currency?
In many cases, yes. As an independent agent on a direct contract with the developer within a Singapore group with international banking infrastructure, we can arrange settlement in your own jurisdiction and currency where available — subject to KYC/AML, source-of-funds verification and applicable law.
