Project overview

Lower entry. Steady long-stay demand.

Title Vivi is a compact rental-focused condominium in Layan — the coastline directly north of Bang Tao. The territory is quieter than the Bang Tao centre but enjoys stable rental demand from long-stay expats and remote-working tenants.

Layouts are 27–29 sqm, designed for rental performance rather than owner occupation. Tenure is registered leasehold, 30+30+30 mechanism, signed direct with the developer.

This project is the lower-entry-ticket option in our catalog — suitable for buyers building a multi-unit portfolio or testing the Phuket market before larger commitments.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Can a foreigner buy here?
Foreign buyers can typically own condominium units under Thailand's freehold foreign-ownership quota, or hold a villa via registered leasehold or a Thai company structure. We confirm the exact path for this project and structure each purchase with documentation and legal review.
What is the starting price and what is included?
We quote the developer's current list price and the furniture/finish package on request — there is no separate buyer-side agency markup. Exact pricing depends on unit, floor and availability.
How do payment and completion work?
A reservation deposit secures the unit, followed by a contract payment and milestone-linked instalments per the developer schedule, with the balance at handover. We share the full schedule and completion date in the dossier.
Can I pay in my own country and currency?
In many cases, yes. As the developer's official agent within a Singapore group with international banking infrastructure, we can arrange settlement in your own jurisdiction and currency where available — subject to KYC/AML, source-of-funds verification and applicable law.
