2026 private allocation round — expressions of interest are open.

Participation is limited, non-public and subject to project availability, KYC/AML, suitability review and signed private documentation. There is no public subscription window and no fixed yield advertised on this page.

What is being prepared

Following the 2025 pilot cycle, the 2026 round focuses on selected Phuket resort real-estate opportunities under documented developer relationships, with the same disciplined due-diligence and KYC/AML approach.

Specific projects, structure, allocation periods and economic terms are part of private documentation. They are shared only after a counterparty has cleared onboarding.
