Introduction

Terra Intelligence Lab (TIL) introduces a missing layer of the AI ecosystem: a continuously-learning territorial world model that bridges AI systems with the physical world.

TIL is built as a full-stack intelligence engine. We are not building yet another analytics product. We are building an infrastructure layer that other AI systems can stand on.

TIL is not a dashboard or analytic tool. It is AI-native infrastructure for territorial understanding — the intelligence layer for global resort economies.

Layer 1 — World Model Engine

Digital Territorial Twin

Multimodal Model

Combining geospatial, social, demand, real estate, legal, developer, and infrastructure signals. Every territory becomes a structured, queryable digital representation.

GRID Index

Dynamic scoring of micro-locations showing demand, competitive pressure, appreciation momentum, and risk accumulation. The GRID Index transforms raw territorial data into actionable intelligence.

Layer 2 — Prediction & Simulation

TIL simulates territorial futures: prices, rental absorption, developer cycles, regulatory scenarios, risk-adjusted ROI, and stress tests.

The model provides forward-looking intelligence, not just historical analysis. It predicts how territories will evolve, where demand will shift, and where risk will accumulate.
