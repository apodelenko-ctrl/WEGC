## Context

The current ACRA Bizfile entry for the company shows the primary activity as:

| Field | Current (to be replaced) |
|---|---|
| SSIC | **46900** — *Wholesale trade of a variety of goods without a dominant product* |

The actual operating model of the company, as described publicly on
[wegc.fund/corporate.html](https://wegc.fund/corporate.html) and applied since
incorporation, is:

1. **Property advisory and real-estate access** — curated coverage of selected
   Phuket resort real-estate projects, developer-direct contracts, due
   diligence, transaction support.
2. **Private real-asset access** — non-public participation framework for a
   limited number of private clients in selected real-estate opportunities,
   subject to KYC/AML, suitability review and signed private documentation.

The company is a private company limited by shares (members ≤ 50), does not
invite the public to subscribe for shares or to deposit money, is **not** a
MAS-licensed fund manager and **not** a financial adviser, and does not
operate a public collective investment scheme.

## Recommended SSIC alignment

Please confirm the appropriate SSIC code(s) and file the change with ACRA.
Suggested candidates for review by counsel/secretary:

| Candidate SSIC | Description | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| **68101** | Real estate developers (residential and commercial) | Closest to property project work, but we are not a developer ourselves |
| **68201** | Real estate activities on a fee or contract basis (real estate agencies) | **Strong candidate** — matches advisory / agency work |
| **70201** | Business and management consultancy | Secondary fit for advisory mandates |
| **64300** | Trusts, funds and similar financial entities | **Avoid** — implies regulated fund activity which we do not conduct |
| **66302** | Fund management activities (regulated) | **Avoid** — requires MAS licence which we do not hold |

**Recommended primary:** `68201` — *Real estate activities on a fee or contract basis*
**Recommended secondary (if applicable):** `70201` — *Business and management consultancy*

The secretary/counsel should confirm before filing.
