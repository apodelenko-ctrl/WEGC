What is TIL?

Terra Intelligence Lab (TIL) introduces a missing layer of the AI ecosystem: a continuously-learning territorial world model that bridges AI systems with the physical world.

TIL is built as a full-stack intelligence engine. We analyze resort territories as living systems: people flows, demand patterns, infrastructure, projects, sentiment and risk — all fused into a single, structured view.

This is not another map. It is an intelligence layer that can be used by both humans and AI agents to make better decisions.
