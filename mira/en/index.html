The idea behind MIRA

A local agent’s relationships.
A wider world of property.

Clients already trust their local agents. MIRA helps those agents offer overseas homes with the information, developer access and practical support they need to move a transaction forward.

We aggregate property proposals across international markets and coordinate current availability, buyer registration, viewings, documentation and payment-support enquiries.

We are building a network of local agencies and independent agents across Russia and other CIS markets, with an initial target of 50–60 agencies.

For property developers

A coordinated channel to local agency relationships, with WEST EAST TRADE GROUP PTE. LTD., Singapore, as your proposed contracting partner.

For local agencies and independent agents

Access to overseas projects and developer-funded commissions, while keeping the client relationship and working under their own brand.

For buyers

Help with choosing a property, obtaining an up-to-date proposal and progressing towards a direct purchase agreement with the developer.

For developer partners

Your projects.
Closer to the right clients.

We make your proposals easier for local agents to understand, present and follow through.

01 / PRESENTATION

A clear place in our marketplace

Project pages, approved images and materials, and information agents can use in a client conversation. We coordinate updates with your sales team.

02 / BUYER INTRODUCTIONS

Registered, coordinated enquiries

We clarify each buyer’s requirements and follow your process for registration, duplicate checks, availability and viewings.

03 / COOPERATION

One commercial point of contact

Our Singapore company coordinates the developer relationship and agency agreement. Buyer registration and commission terms are agreed before introductions begin.

04 / COMPLETION

Support beyond the introduction

We help coordinate the buyer’s direct contract with the developer, signatures and original documents. Local legal consultations and payment support can be arranged according to the transaction.

A shared way of working

From the first conversation
to a completed transaction.

We begin with your agency requirements. We exchange company information, agree the scope of cooperation and confirm how introductions, materials and commissions will be handled.

The introducing agency or independent agent earns the remuneration agreed for its role. Developer commission, allocation and payment arrangements are documented before work begins.

Each buyer is introduced individually, following your registration and due-diligence requirements.

The transaction, step by step

  • Confirm the partnership and approved project materials.
  • Understand the buyer’s brief and register the introduction.
  • Request a current proposal and coordinate a viewing.
  • Support the direct buyer–developer agreement and document exchange.
  • Coordinate payment support where agreed, and the commission process.
