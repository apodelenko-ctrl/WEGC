Why this project

A product we already know — in a market that still misses it

Our project lead, Artem Podelenko, first discovered SARASA olives in Kaliningrad (Russia), then again in Kuala Lumpur. After moving to Phuket, he was surprised that a product he had grown to love was simply not available in Thailand.

Good olives are scarce here, often overpriced, and rarely match the flavour of authentic Spanish aliñadas. We believe SARASA can fill that gap.

Thailand has a large community of expats, wine lovers and premium-food buyers. Spain already dominates olive imports. The category exists — but this specific product line does not. That is the opportunity.
