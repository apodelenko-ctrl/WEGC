Bringing authentic Spanish table olives to Thailand

WET Solutions Limited is preparing import and distribution of premium SARASA olives — seasoned Verdial and related lines — for Villa Market, Tops, Gourmet Market and the HORECA channel across Thailand and Phuket.

Contact export team Main website

Who we are

WET Solutions Limited (Hong Kong SAR, CR No. 76458883) is an international B2B food-trading company: supplier sourcing, export operations, shipment documentation and cold-chain logistics across Asia.

Our team is based in Phuket, Thailand, with operational experience in Canada, GCC, Southeast Asia and CIS markets. We work with licensed importers, customs brokers and premium retail buyers.

Project lead: Artem Podelenko — also founder of HERD Research (Phuket).

Why Thailand — and why now

  • Large expat community and wine-oriented consumers on Phuket and in Bangkok
  • Premium olive selection is limited; prices are high
  • SARASA is not currently listed in Thai retail — first-mover opportunity
  • Spain already leads olive imports into Thailand; demand for Mediterranean food is established
  • Target launch ahead of high season (Oct–Apr tourist flow)

Products we plan to import

Initial focus: retail cans (0.37 L and related formats) from the SARASA range — mixed trial pallets, then container scale if sell-through is confirmed.

Hechizos del Sur Green Verdial · seasoned (aliñada) · cracked-style flavour
La Picantona Spicy seasoned Verdial
Morenas del Sur Black Cacereña · pitted · seasoned

We are in discussion with the producer and seek an exclusive Thailand distribution arrangement. Thai-language sticker labels will be applied before import, in compliance with Thai FDA requirements.

Distribution plan

  • Premium retail: Villa Market, Tops / Central Food Hall, Gourmet Market, Foodland
  • HORECA: Mediterranean restaurants, tapas bars, hotels, wine bars
  • Phuket & Bangkok first, then wider Thailand
  • In-store tastings and promotional campaigns at launch

What we handle

  • Import contract & payment (HK / SG banking)
  • Thai FDA import licensing pathway (via Thai company)
  • Thai label artwork & factory-applied stickers
  • Customs clearance & bonded logistics (with licensed broker)
  • Retail listing & field sales (local distributor partners)
