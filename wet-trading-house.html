How We Operate

WET SOLUTIONS LIMITED is a Hong Kong Principal Trading House. We operate as a direct counterparty in cross-border trade. We purchase goods from suppliers and resell them to clients under our own contracts. We assume full responsibility for execution, logistics and documentation.

We act as a Principal, not an intermediary

We contract directly with suppliers
We purchase goods under our own trade terms
We resell to clients under defined commercial agreements
We deliver goods with full shipping and compliance documentation execution
We maintain internal control over suppliers, logistics and documentation at each stage of execution
