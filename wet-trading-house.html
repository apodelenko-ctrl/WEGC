About the Company

WET SOLUTION LIMITED is a Hong Kong–registered trading house specializing in international B2B distribution. We work with vetted suppliers across Asia, sourcing textile goods and frozen meat products, and coordinating deliveries to importers and distributors in multiple global markets.

From our base in Hong Kong, we manage contract terms, documentation, quality control, and logistics — providing transparent pricing and reliable commercial execution for every transaction. Our expertise spans supply chain coordination, regulatory compliance, and cross-border trade facilitation.

Business Scope

Our company consolidates supply from factories across Asia and structures deliveries to Africa, Asia, and Eastern Europe under clear documentation and commercial standards. We operate as a bridge between Asian manufacturers and global B2B buyers, ensuring quality, compliance, and timely delivery.
