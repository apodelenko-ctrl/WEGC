About the Company

WET SOLUTION LIMITED operates as a Hong Kong–registered trading house specializing in international B2B distribution. We work with vetted suppliers across Asia, source textile goods and frozen meat products, and coordinate deliveries to importers and distributors in multiple global markets.

From Hong Kong we manage contract terms, documentation, quality control, and logistics — providing transparent pricing and reliable commercial execution for every transaction.

Business Scope

Our company consolidates supply from factories in Asia and structures deliveries to Africa, Asia and Eastern Europe under clear documentation and commercial standards.

  • Wholesale textile sourcing.
  • Frozen meat & poultry distribution.
  • Contract negotiation & documentation support.
  • Supply chain coordination & logistics.
