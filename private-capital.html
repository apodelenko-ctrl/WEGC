Private participation. By request only.

West East Gate Capital structures private participation in selected Phuket real-estate opportunities for a limited number of private clients. Participation is not publicly offered and is available only after onboarding, KYC/AML and execution of private documentation.

Important. West East Gate Capital is not a MAS-licensed fund manager or financial adviser. Nothing on this page is a public offer, investment advice, deposit-taking, or a guarantee of return. No participation terms, returns or economics are disclosed publicly. All economics are deal-specific and shared only in private documentation, after KYC/AML and suitability review.
How this works

From expression of interest to private documentation.

A private capital relationship begins with a brief expression of interest. From there, we run KYC/AML, suitability review and confirm whether your case fits our coverage. Only after that step do we share private materials.

Participation is non-public, limited in size, and subject to project availability and to signed legal documentation. We are deliberately not a retail platform.

Public language we deliberately avoid

No fixed yield, no guaranteed return, no “invest now” button, no public IRR figures, no public minimum ticket. Specific economics — preferred return, allocation periods, structure — are part of private documentation only and subject to applicable law and signed agreements.
