Buyer Intelligence FAQ — TIL answers the hard questions

Foreigners can own condominium units (up to 49% of a building) in freehold, but cannot own land directly. Leasehold is common for villas and land plots, typically 30-year renewable leases. However, leasehold renewal is not guaranteed and carries long-term depreciation risk. TIL models the probability of successful renewal based on developer track record, legal structure, and historical patterns.

Leasehold carries structural depreciation risk. As the lease term decreases, the property value typically declines. Renewal is not automatic and depends on the lessor's willingness and legal structure. TIL's risk-score approach evaluates developer reliability, lease structure, renewal probability, and historical renewal rates in similar projects. We factor in these risks when scoring projects in the GRID Index.

Daily rental of condominium units requires a hotel license, which is difficult to obtain and rarely granted to individual unit owners. Most "daily rental" operations operate in a legal gray area. Enforcement varies by district and can change with new regulations. TIL models regulatory enforcement probability based on district-level enforcement history, project scale, and operator compliance track record. We flag high-risk projects where daily rental claims may not be legally sustainable.

Brochure numbers often claim 6-8% gross yields, but real net yields after management fees, maintenance, vacancies, and seasonal variations typically range from 3-5% for well-managed properties. TIL uses AI-adjusted occupancy forecasts based on actual booking data, seasonal patterns, operator quality, and location dynamics. We provide realistic yield projections, not marketing numbers.

Use GRID Index — a structured scoring model based on thousands of territorial signals. GRID evaluates location dynamics, developer risk, project viability, financial projections, and exit liquidity. Each project receives a composite score across multiple dimensions, allowing objective comparison beyond marketing claims. TIL reports include GRID scores and detailed breakdowns for any project you're evaluating.

Resale liquidity varies significantly by project, location, and market cycle. Premium locations with strong rental track records have better liquidity, while off-plan or secondary locations can take months or years to sell. TIL's AI exit timing windows analyze market cycles, liquidity depth, seasonal patterns, and territory dynamics to predict optimal exit windows. We help you understand not just what to buy, but when to exit.
