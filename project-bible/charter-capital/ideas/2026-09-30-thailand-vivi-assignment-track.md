## Context

Working concept discussed for Charter Capital: use a real under-construction Thai condominium contractual right as an operational asset in a documented capital-mobility / property-investment workflow, rather than buying and registering a completed condominium for each case.

The existing asset identified for analysis is the already purchased **The Title VIVI unit VBA312 / A312**. The project remains under construction, so the relevant instrument is assignment of the purchaser's contractual rights before completion.

This note records the idea for retrieval by local agents. It is not an approved legal/compliance conclusion and must not be represented as a guarantee of bank acceptance.

## Contract finding — Clause 4.7

The available VIVI purchase agreement contains an assignment provision in Clause 4.7.

Substance of the clause:
- Purchaser is entitled to assign the rights under the agreement to another person.
- Purchaser must notify the Seller in writing.
- Purchaser pays an **administration fee of THB 100,000 per contract**.
- Assignment to mother, father, spouse, relative children, or the Purchaser's statutory heirs is stated to be free of charge.
- The transferee takes all rights and obligations of the Purchaser under the agreement with the Seller.

The clause, as reviewed, does **not expressly state a one-assignment limit**. Do not infer from silence that repeated assignments are automatically permitted. Confirm with Rhom Bho Property / developer in writing whether A→B→C successive assignments of the same unit are accepted, and whether THB 100,000 is charged on each assignment.

## Proposed operational idea

1. Start from the existing contractual position in VIVI VBA312 / A312.
2. Use assignment of the real purchaser rights instead of registering a completed unit and reselling it.
3. A client acquiring the position must genuinely become the holder of the contractual rights and obligations.
4. A subsequent exit would be another real assignment to an actual buyer, if permitted by the developer and applicable law.
5. Preserve the complete documentary and payment audit trail for every leg.

The operational objective is to reduce unnecessary title-registration / completed-property handling while retaining a real property transaction and a documented chain of rights.

## Items to validate before implementation

- Written developer confirmation that repeated/successive assignments are permitted.
- Whether the THB 100,000 administration fee applies to every successive assignment.
- Required developer forms, signatures, KYC and timing for an assignment.
- Whether any installments must be paid before assignment.
- Whether an assignment can occur while an installment is pending.
- Whether the developer can refuse a proposed assignee and on what contractual basis.
- Thai tax/stamp-duty/withholding/VAT consequences, if any, for each party and each assignment.
- Accounting treatment for the Singapore company and the client.
- Exact payment routing and documentary evidence required for each real transaction.
- Sanctions/AML/Source-of-Funds review based on full disclosure of the complete capital path.
- Exit/liquidity risk if no independent buyer is available when required.
