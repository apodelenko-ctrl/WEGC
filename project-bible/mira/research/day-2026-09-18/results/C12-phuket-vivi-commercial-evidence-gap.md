## Exact commercial gaps to close privately

1. **Agreement continuity.** Reconcile the current agreement term with any later renewal/addendum. If none exists, obtain renewal before relying on the relationship after the current term. Do not infer automatic renewal.
2. **Current sellable inventory.** Obtain developer-issued current stock/inventory for VIVI with timestamp/version. A public project page is not inventory.
3. **Current price/payment plan.** Use the developer's current broker pack/quotation source. Do not carry old catalogue numbers forward.
4. **Lead registration and protection.** Record exact submission channel, required fields, acknowledgement, protection period, duplicate/conflict rules, attribution and rejection/expiry behavior.
5. **Current contracting/seller mapping.** Confirm the entity named in the buyer contract for the exact unit/tenure path; do not infer every buyer-side seller field from the agency agreement alone.
6. **Partner-agency model.** Establish whether WET may work through a downstream MIRA agency, what evidence is required, who registers the customer and how the downstream share is documented. The WET developer agreement alone does not prove a universal MIRA 90/10 or any other split.
7. **Media/material rights.** Obtain the exact current broker asset pack and written scope for MIRA plus downstream agency use: channels, edits, attribution, expiry and project/phase coverage.
8. **Commission operations.** Keep the confidential rate/trigger/payout/tax/invoice fields in the private CRM and test the operational checklist against the signed agreement. Public GitHub stores only `evidenced_private=true` and expiry/renewal hold status.
9. **Buyer funds.** Preserve direct-to-developer handling required by the private evidence. Any separate payment-support service must not silently change the developer contract/payment destination.
10. **Buyer documents and legal review.** Keep buyer contracts, passports, banking data and signed transaction documents outside this research branch. Confirm tenure/legal-document version at the actual transaction stage.
11. **Construction/progress claims.** The first-party VIVI page currently shows project-progress fields. Treat them as a dated website observation, not a handover guarantee.
