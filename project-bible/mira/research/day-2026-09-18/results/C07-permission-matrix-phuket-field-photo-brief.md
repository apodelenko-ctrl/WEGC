## A. Permission request: standard scope to ask for

Every request must identify the **exact project and, where relevant, phase/collection**, and must ask for a named/current asset set rather than “permission to use your website.” Ask the rightsholder/licensor to confirm in writing:

1. asset filenames/pack or a stable approved download route;
2. rightsholder/licensor for each asset set, including any hospitality/architecture/brand layer;
3. permitted MIRA channels: public catalogue, passworded agency cabinet, email/newsletter, social, PDF/presentation and exhibition screen/print;
4. whether partner agencies may download, forward, repost or add their own branding;
5. territories and languages;
6. attribution/logo/trademark rules;
7. permitted resize/crop/compression/translation/captioning and whether visual editing is restricted;
8. duration, expiry, revocation/replacement procedure and required removal period;
9. whether archived/historical use remains permitted after replacement;
10. written evidence/contact role sufficient to prove the grant later.

Do **not** ask for or imply exclusivity, sales appointment, current inventory, buyer-registration authority or commercial terms in a media-rights request unless those are intentionally handled in a separate commercial conversation.

# C. Phuket field-photography brief — own-content path

## Objective

Build a small library of **MIRA-owned or explicitly licensed current field content** for the first real Phuket supply projects so the marketplace is not dependent on uncertain developer website images. The brief is ready before a project is selected; selection/contract/media-access status is a separate commercial gate.

## Pre-shoot gate — all must be explicit

Before entering or photographing a project, record:

- exact project, phase/building/unit/showroom/common area to be covered;
- who granted site/property access and what spaces/times are allowed;
- whether construction areas require escort, PPE or are prohibited;
- whether interiors/show units/common areas may be photographed for **commercial B2B publication**, not merely visited;
- signage, artwork, architectural render, screen, plan, document or third-party brand restrictions;
- whether identifiable staff/visitors/residents may appear; default is to avoid identifiable people unless a release/authorisation is obtained;
- whether the developer permits partner agencies to reuse the finished MIRA material and under which credit/branding rules;
- a private evidence reference for the permission. Personal releases/contracts stay outside public GitHub.

A sales invitation or access to a showroom is **not** by itself a commercial media licence.

## Required shot families

Capture only authorised areas and label every family with exact project/phase/date:

1. **Identity / verification:** entrance, project name/signage and one context shot that lets an editor verify the location without exposing private documents.
2. **Exterior / approach:** street arrival, façade/massing, pedestrian context and immediate surroundings as actually observed.
3. **Common areas / amenities:** only completed/accessible areas; captions distinguish existing from render/planned content.
4. **Show unit / interior:** only the exact authorised show unit or completed unit; never imply that staging/layout equals every saleable unit.
5. **Construction status:** only if site permission and safe access exist; record date and exact building/phase. Do not infer completion percentage from photographs.
6. **Neighbourhood context:** public-road/street context useful to an agency; do not present unrelated beach/landmark imagery as if it were inside the development.
7. **Short video:** slow establishing/context clips and factual walk-through; no invented voice-over about price, return, inventory or legal status.

## Deliverable structure

For every selected frame/clip retain:

- original file and a web derivative;
- internal project ID + exact phase/location label + capture date;
- photographer/source and rights-evidence reference;
- visual-QA state (`captured`, `reviewed_exact`, `publication_approved`) kept separate;
- caption restricted to directly observed/source-verified facts;
- notes for crop/brand/person restrictions.

Preferred publication crops can include landscape, square/portrait and vertical social derivatives, but crops must not remove required credits, notices or material context. Do not remove watermarks from third-party material.

## Drone footage — separate gate, not a default shot

Current CAAT guidance says camera-equipped drones require registration; its public guidance directs operators to register the aircraft and remote pilot through the **UAS Portal** and obtain the separate NBTC radio-frequency permission. The official UAS Portal also provides flight-permit guidance. Sources rechecked 18 Sep 2026:

- https://www.caat.or.th/en/do-you-know/171528/
- https://www.caat.or.th/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Important-things-to-know-before-using-drones-ENG.pdf
- https://uasportal.caat.or.th/

Therefore: **no drone launch is included in this brief by default**. Before each planned flight, verify current CAAT/UAS Portal rules, required registration/flight permission and site/local restrictions for the exact location/date; confirm operator/aircraft credentials privately; obtain property/site permission; then record the permitted flight scope. Historical or expired temporary restrictions are not treated as current merely because they remain searchable.

## Safety / privacy defaults

- No unsupervised entry into construction zones or restricted roofs/plant rooms.
- Do not photograph passports, buyer files, sales screens, access badges, unit-owner records, licence plates or other unnecessary personal/operational data.
- Avoid identifiable people by default; if a person is the subject, obtain a suitable release/authority and keep the evidence privately.
- Do not stage a worker/resident as a buyer, investor or representative.
- Never describe a photographed unit as available unless current supply evidence separately supports that claim.
- Weather, temporary works, landscaping and construction state are date-specific observations, not permanent project promises.

## Field package acceptance checklist

A field package is ready for LOCAL only when each selected asset has: exact project/phase match; source/capture date; visual QA; permission/right chain; privacy/safety review; no unsupported commercial caption; and an explicit channel scope. If any one is absent, keep a labelled cover or internal hold rather than force publication.
